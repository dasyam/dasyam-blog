Refund Policy

Last updated 26 July 2026.

What you're paying for

The program fee covers your first seven days: one onboarding call, a short evening check-in, and one small step each night on WhatsApp. There is no auto-renewal, and nothing further is charged without your agreement.

Refund window

If you change your mind before your onboarding call has taken place, you can request a full refund, no questions asked.

Once the onboarding call has happened and your program has started, the fee covers time and a personal decision made for that week, so it isn't refundable from that point.

If, during the screening questions, it turns out the program isn't a fit for you and you were charged anyway, you'll get a full refund automatically.

How to request one

Message us on WhatsApp, the same number the program messages you from, and let us know you'd like a refund. We'll process eligible refunds within 5 to 7 business days to your original payment method.
